MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Master Services Agreement (the "Agreement") is made on 10 September 2026 between Northwind Technologies Ltd ("Northwind") and Acme Cloud Services Ltd (the "Supplier").

1. Services. The Supplier shall provide the cloud hosting and support services described in the order forms agreed between the parties from time to time.

2. Fees and Payment. Northwind shall pay each undisputed invoice within twenty (20) days of receipt of that invoice.

3. Fee Increases. The Supplier may increase the fees at any time during the term by giving Northwind thirty (30) days' written notice.

4. Renewal. This Agreement renews automatically for successive two (2) year terms unless Northwind gives written notice at least ninety (90) days before the end of the then-current term.

5. Intellectual Property. All intellectual property rights in the deliverables created under this Agreement shall remain with the Supplier.

6. Data Use. The Supplier may use data submitted by Northwind to train and improve its artificial-intelligence models.

7. Security Incidents. The Supplier shall notify Northwind of any security incident affecting Northwind data within five (5) business days of becoming aware of it.

8. Data Location. The Supplier shall store and process Northwind personal data only within the United Kingdom or the European Economic Area.

9. Warranty. The services are provided 'as is' without warranty of any kind.

10. Limitation of Liability. The Supplier's total liability under this Agreement shall be limited to the fees paid in the three (3) months before the claim arose.

11. Subcontracting. The Supplier shall not subcontract a material part of the services without Northwind's prior written consent and shall remain fully liable for its subcontractors.

12. Termination for Breach. Northwind may terminate this Agreement for material breach only after a one hundred and twenty (120) day remedy period has expired without the breach being remedied.

13. Standard Terms. The Supplier's standard terms and conditions apply to the services and prevail over this Agreement where they conflict.

14. Governing Law. This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of England and Wales.
