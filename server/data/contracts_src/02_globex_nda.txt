NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

This Non-Disclosure Agreement (the "Agreement") is made on 15 April 2026 between Northwind Technologies Ltd ("Northwind") and Globex Corporation (the "Counterparty").

1. Purpose. The parties intend to evaluate a joint product offering for enterprise customers (the "Purpose") and will exchange confidential information solely for that Purpose.

2. Definition of Confidential Information. Confidential Information means any non-public information, in any form, that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential, whether or not it is marked as such.

3. Confidentiality Obligations. The Receiving Party shall keep the Confidential Information secure, restrict access to personnel who need to know it, and use it only for the Purpose.

4. Term of Confidentiality. The obligations of confidentiality shall survive for a period of five (5) years after termination of this Agreement, except that obligations in respect of trade secrets shall continue for as long as the information remains a trade secret.

5. Compelled Disclosure. The Receiving Party may disclose Confidential Information to the extent that it is required to do so by law or by order of a court or government authority.

6. Personal Data. Either party may share personal data of its employees and customers with the other party where reasonably necessary for the Purpose.

7. Non-Solicitation. During the term of this Agreement and for two (2) years afterwards, neither party shall solicit for employment any employee of the other party who was involved in the Purpose.

8. Remedies. Each party waives any right to seek injunctive or equitable relief in respect of a breach of this Agreement and agrees that monetary damages are the sole remedy.

9. Publicity. The Counterparty may reference Northwind as a customer in its marketing materials and press releases during the term of this Agreement.

10. Audit Rights. The Counterparty may, on reasonable notice, conduct on-site audits of Northwind's facilities to verify compliance with this Agreement.

11. Governing Law. This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of England and Wales.

12. Termination. Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience by giving the other party thirty (30) days' written notice.

13. Return of Information. On termination, the Receiving Party shall return or destroy all Confidential Information within thirty (30) days.

14. Entire Agreement. This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties regarding its subject matter and supersedes all prior discussions, whether written or oral.
