MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (the "Agreement") is made on 2 June 2026 between Northwind Technologies Ltd ("Northwind") and Initech Solutions LLC (the "Counterparty").

1. Purpose. The parties wish to discuss a possible reseller arrangement (the "Purpose") and will disclose confidential information to each other for that Purpose only.

2. Definition of Confidential Information. Confidential Information means any non-public information that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential, whether or not it is marked as confidential at the time of disclosure.

3. Confidentiality Obligations. Each party shall protect the Confidential Information it receives with at least reasonable care and shall use it solely for the Purpose.

4. Term of Confidentiality. The obligations of confidentiality shall survive for a period of three (3) years after termination or expiry of this Agreement. Obligations in respect of trade secrets shall continue perpetually for so long as the information remains a trade secret under applicable law.

5. Compelled Disclosure. If the Receiving Party is required by law or by order of a court to disclose Confidential Information, it shall give the Disclosing Party prompt prior written notice and shall cooperate in seeking a protective order.

6. Personal Data. Where personal data is shared under this Agreement, each party shall comply with all applicable data protection laws and shall enter into a data processing agreement where one is required.

7. Limitation of Liability. Each party's aggregate liability under this Agreement shall not exceed USD 100,000, and neither party shall be liable for indirect or consequential damages.

8. Governing Law. This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Delaware, USA.

9. Termination. Either party may terminate this Agreement at any time by giving the other party sixty (60) days' written notice.

10. Return of Information. Within thirty (30) days of written request, the Receiving Party shall return or destroy all Confidential Information in its possession.

11. Exclusivity. During the term of this Agreement, Northwind agrees to deal exclusively with the Counterparty in respect of the Purpose and shall not engage in discussions with any competitor of the Counterparty.

12. Assignment. Neither party may assign this Agreement or any right under it without the prior written consent of the other party, except to a successor in a merger.

13. Entire Agreement. This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties regarding its subject matter and supersedes all prior discussions, whether written or oral.
